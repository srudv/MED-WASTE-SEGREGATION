# Problem Statement: Medical Waste Data Analysis and Management

## Background
Medical facilities generate multiple categories of waste that require appropriate segregation and handling. Data about waste categories and related characteristics can help teams understand the composition of available records and communicate patterns more clearly.

## Problem
Medical-waste datasets may contain missing values, duplicate records, inconsistent category names, or fields that are difficult to compare directly. When the data is not standardized and analyzed systematically, it becomes harder to produce reliable summaries and accessible visual reports.

## Proposed Solution
Create a documented workflow that collects relevant, shareable medical-waste data, checks and preprocesses it, analyzes attributes supported by the dataset, and presents the results through charts and an interactive dashboard.

## Objectives
- Organize relevant medical-waste data and record its provenance.
- Improve data quality through documented preprocessing.
- Summarize waste categories and other available attributes.
- Communicate findings with clear visualizations.
- Provide interactive exploration through a dashboard.
- Make the workflow reproducible for future contributors.

## Guiding Questions
- What categories and attributes are present in the selected dataset?
- How are records distributed across the available categories?
- Which data-quality issues need to be addressed before analysis?
- Which charts best communicate the findings without misrepresenting the data?
- Can users explore the resulting metrics through dashboard filters?

## Constraints
The results depend on the quality, coverage, licensing, and scope of the chosen dataset. The project will not infer real-world hospital-wide trends from an unrepresentative dataset, and it will not provide clinical or regulatory decisions.

## Expected Outcome
A reproducible dataset workflow, documented analytical findings, clear visualizations, and an interactive dashboard grounded in the collected data.
